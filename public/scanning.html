GOVERNED MEASUREMENT // OPT OUT HONORED BEFORE TRAFFIC

LeakyCompute sends bounded read-only requests only to fresh candidates nominated by public indexes, plus hosted self-checks of the caller's own public address. If you do not want your address space included, say so. You do not have to explain why. Exclusions become active when received, block new permits, do not expire, and trigger verified deletion of retained records and attempt state.

Open a removal request

Prefer not to use GitHub? Email works and carries exactly the same weight — see the contact on the maintainer's profile. Needing an account in order to be left alone would not be an opt-out.

What probes send

Every request is a read-only GET against a reviewed version, health, or listing endpoint. It checks whether a service answers an unauthenticated request and never sends an action to prove what that access would allow.

Probes never submitted a job, pulled a model, sent a prompt, started a kernel, read a file, or attempted to authenticate. They did not follow redirects or scan ports; each service was contacted only on a reviewed port.

Traffic uses the User-Agent LeakyCompute-SafeProbe. A durable cooldown lease is written before target traffic, and the Worker consumes a one-time permit immediately before opening an address-pinned socket.

Where probes come from. Candidate nomination and scheduling run on GitHub-hosted Actions runners, but target requests originate from the Cloudflare Worker socket runtime. Cloudflare uses shared egress pools, so the User-Agent—not reverse DNS—is the project identifier.

How your host came to our attention

We do not find hosts by scanning or walking neighboring address space. Every address contacted must carry a public-index observation no more than seven days old, or belong to an approved owner request. A retained historical row without that timestamp is not permission to contact it again.

This also means opting out here does not remove you from those indexes. Shodan and Censys are separate services with their own removal processes. And if a host is genuinely exposed, opting out of our measurement does not close it — the people who are not asking permission will still find it.

What we keep

Retained rolling records may contain address, port, service, version string, first and last seen, and geo/ASN metadata. We do not retain model lists, job records, or page contents. Records expire 180 days after last contact. Raw addresses are never published; public figures are aggregate counts by country, ASN, and stack.

If you are exposed

The fastest fixes, in order: bind the service to 127.0.0.1 instead of 0.0.0.0; restrict the port at your host firewall and cloud security group; put an authenticating reverse proxy in front if you need remote access. Use the local defensive CLI from infrastructure you control. It keeps results on your machine.

Rules we hold ourselves to

The project's invariants are written down and versioned, so this page can be checked against the code rather than taken on trust: SECURITY.md. If you find a way for a probe path to bypass these rules, that is a security issue in our software and we want to hear about it — open a private advisory on the repository.
