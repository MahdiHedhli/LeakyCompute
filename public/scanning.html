ACTIVE PROBING SUSPENDED // OPT OUT STILL HONORED

LeakyCompute currently sends no active discovery or hosted-check traffic. If you do not want your address space included in any future measurement, say so now. You do not have to explain why. Exclusions are applied when we receive them, do not expire, and also trigger deletion of retained records.

Open a removal request

Prefer not to use GitHub? Email works and carries exactly the same weight — see the contact on the maintainer's profile. Needing an account in order to be left alone would not be an opt-out.

What historical probes sent

Active measurement is suspended. When it operated, every request was a read-only GET against a version, health, or listing endpoint. It checked whether a service answered an unauthenticated request and never sent an action to prove what that access would allow.

Probes never submitted a job, pulled a model, sent a prompt, started a kernel, read a file, or attempted to authenticate. They did not follow redirects or scan ports; each service was contacted only on a reviewed port.

Historical traffic used the User-Agent LeakyCompute-SafeProbe. If active measurement is ever redesigned and re-enabled, attributable traffic remains a requirement and this page will be updated before it starts.

Where historical probes came from. Completed runs used maintainer systems and GitHub-hosted Actions runners; the hosted checker was designed for Cloudflare Workers but is disabled because that platform cannot reliably fetch IP-literal targets. These are shared source pools, so the User-Agent—not reverse DNS—was the project identifier. Any future source architecture must be documented here before traffic resumes.

How your host came to our attention

We did not find hosts by scanning. Every address historically contacted already appeared in a public index such as Shodan or was supplied by its owner. Current internet measurement reads public records and sends the listed hosts no traffic.

This also means opting out here does not remove you from those indexes. Shodan and Censys are separate services with their own removal processes. And if a host is genuinely exposed, opting out of our measurement does not close it — the people who are not asking permission will still find it.

What we keep

Retained historical records may contain address, port, service, version string, first and last seen, and geo/ASN metadata. We do not retain model lists, job records, or page contents. Records expire 180 days after last contact. Raw addresses are never published; public figures are aggregate counts by country, ASN, and stack.

If you are exposed

The fastest fixes, in order: bind the service to 127.0.0.1 instead of 0.0.0.0; restrict the port at your host firewall and cloud security group; put an authenticating reverse proxy in front if you need remote access. Use the local defensive CLI from infrastructure you control. It keeps results on your machine.

Rules we hold ourselves to

The project's invariants are written down and versioned, so this page can be checked against the code rather than taken on trust: SECURITY.md. If you find a way for a dormant or future probe path to bypass these rules, that is a security issue in our software and we want to hear about it — open a private advisory on the repository.
